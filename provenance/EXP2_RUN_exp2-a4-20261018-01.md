## Why ramp 1 missed the coverage rule

Four cores running the P4c job back to back heat the die from about 46 °C to 70 °C in 12–23 s, which is
about 60–120 jobs spread over 12 bins. Below 70 °C, each 2 °C bin therefore holds 1–17 jobs and is dropped
(A4 keeps bins with at least 20 jobs). In ramp 0 the climb from 64 °C to 72 °C took long enough for four
bins to reach 20–50 jobs. In ramp 1 it was faster: 70 °C after 12.7 s instead of 23.3 s. The likely reason
is that the board and heatsink were still warm from ramp 0, although the die had cooled to 46.7 °C
(cooldown 250 s). Its lowest kept bin was 71.1 °C, 0.3 °C above the limit.

This is a design flaw in A4, not a fault in the run. The fast heating was known from the P=4 diagnostic of
2026-10-17 (about 15 °C in 10 s), but the 20-jobs-per-bin rule was not checked against it. All bins, kept
and dropped, with the time each was first reached: `a4/coverage_descriptive.log`.

- **Shape.** Bin-median latency is 196.4–196.6 ms in every kept bin up to the 78 °C bin, 198.2–198.8 ms in the

  slope.
- **exp2's own ARM-clock knee:** 81.3 °C (Experiment 1: 80.8 °C), depth 14.9 % (1800.5 → 1531.4 MHz).
  Supporting evidence only.
- **Latency against the ARM clock** (jobs with a clock read inside them; observed median vs. predicted from
  the 1800 MHz level by f_ref / f): 1800 MHz 197.9 vs 197.9 ms (n 384); 1726 MHz 209.1 vs 206.4 (281);
  1677 MHz 211.4 vs 212.4 (353); 1629 MHz 213.8 vs 218.7 (270); 1580 MHz 216.3 vs 225.5 (120); 1531 MHz 217.2
  vs 232.7 (10). Latency follows the clock at the first steps and less than proportionally at the lowest
  clocks. One read covers about 1 ms of a 200 ms job, so the read is a sample of a clock that moves during
  the job.
- **Temperature near the watchdog stop.** The die settled at a median of 83.3 °C in both ramps. 2 of about
  7200 telemetry samples (5 Hz) read 85.2 °C, both in ramp 1 at 8–10 min; the watchdog's 5 s readings peaked
  at 84.7 °C. Its 85.0 °C stop was not reached, but a rerun has a real chance of a safety stop on the
  plateau.
- The first job of ramp 0 took 220.6 ms (process warm-up); later jobs 196.3–196.9 ms below the knee.
