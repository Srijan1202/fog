## What A5 fixed, and what stopped this run

- **The coverage problem of run -01 is solved.** The paced warm-up filled every paced bin to its quota:
  - ramp 0: 13 bins from 44 °C to 68 °C, 391 paced jobs, 143 s of idle waits, ended at 221 s;
  - ramp 1: 12 bins from 46 °C to 68 °C, 361 paced jobs, 222 s of waits, ended at 294 s.

  The A4 coverage counts were 13 and 12 kept bins at or below knee − 10 °C, and 3 and 3 at or above the
  knee. This is descriptive: the run is invalid anyway, because it was aborted.
- **Pacing did not change job latency** (descriptive): paced jobs had a median of 196.2 ms (n 752), and
  196.2 ms after an idle wait (n 739). Unpaced jobs below the knee had 196.33 ms (n 350).
- **The 85.0 °C stop.** On the plateau the die sits at a median of about 83.3 °C. Single readings reach
  85.211 °C, the sensor's next step above 84.7 °C.
  - Telemetry (5 Hz) readings ≥ 85.0 °C: ramp 0 at 626 s and 629 s, which the watchdog's 5 s readings
    missed; ramp 1 at 597 s, 664 s and 673 s, the last one read by the watchdog. That is 5.0–6.8 min after
    back-to-back load began.
  - In run -01 they came 8–10 min after the ramp started, and the watchdog missed them.
  - A5 expected the warm-up to reduce the time on the plateau. It did, by 3.7–4.9 min per ramp. That was not
    enough.
- **Everything else held:**
  - 2 of 2 ramps started, 4901 jobs, all complete;
  - no under-voltage at any read;
  - ARM clock readable in 1385 of 1385 reads;
  - exp2's own ARM-clock knee 81.3 °C (supporting evidence only);
  - cleanup verified.

## Proposed next step (for the user's review; not done)

Two ways to finish Experiment 2. Either needs a new amendment, A6, approved by the user:

1. **Shorter back-to-back phase (my recommendation).** End each ramp a fixed 4 min after the paced warm-up
   ends.
   - In both runs the bins above the knee held 141–2474 jobs.
   - The 80 °C and 82 °C bins fill within about 2 min of reaching the knee.
   - No reading ≥ 85.0 °C came earlier than 5.0 min after back-to-back load began, across four ramps.
   - The safety stop stays at 85.0 °C.
2. **Raise the watchdog stop** (for example to 86 °C). The 85.2 °C readings are the firmware regulating
   around its own limit. This is a safety decision for the user, not for Claude.
