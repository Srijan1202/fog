## Descriptive only (not part of G2)

- **Shape.** Bin-median latency is 196.2–196.5 ms in every kept bin from 46 °C to 78 °C in both ramps, then
  197.4–198.0 ms in the 80 °C bins, 207.9–208.5 ms at 82 °C and 211.0–212.1 ms at 84 °C. s(T) is flat below
  the knee and steps up above it. The linear slope summarises a step: about 6 % at 83 °C relative to below the
  knee.
- **Mediation (latency vs ARM clock;** observed median vs predicted from the 1800 MHz level by f_ref / f**):**

  | clock | observed | predicted | n |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1800 MHz | 196.6 ms | 196.6 ms | 334 |
  | 1726 MHz | 207.7 | 205.0 | 107 |
  | 1677 MHz | 210.2 | 211.0 | 88 |
  | 1629 MHz | 212.7 | 217.2 | 63 |
  | 1580 MHz | 215.6 | 223.9 | 26 |

  Latency tracks the clock, less than proportionally at the lowest clocks. One read is a sample of a clock
  that moves during the job.
- **Paced vs unpaced jobs:** median 196.35 ms (n 723; 711 of them after an idle wait) against 196.5 ms for
  unpaced jobs below the knee (n 352).
